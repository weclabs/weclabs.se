Mobile apps for retailers

W.E.C Labs AB is a Swedish software development company based in Stockholm. We develop iPhone and Android apps that strengthen the relationship between stores and their customers.

Apps in the store's name

Our apps are published under the store's own name on the App Store and Google Play. The store keeps its brand and its customer relationships.

Connected to e-commerce

Our apps connect to the store's existing e-commerce platform and customer accounts.

Privacy first

Data is stored in the EU, and we only collect what is needed for the apps to work.

What we do

We handle concept, design, development, hosting, updates and support of mobile apps for retailers, with a focus on beauty and retail.

Are you a retailer and want to know more? Get in touch at kontakt@weclabs.se.
