Mobile apps for beauty and retail businesses

W.E.C Labs AB is a Swedish software development company based in Stockholm. We build iPhone and Android apps that give a store's customers their own place for their products and make it easy to buy again.

All your products in one place

Customers scan the barcode or photograph the package and save the product, shade and purchase date in the app.

Buy again and wishlists

The right variant goes into the cart with one tap. Wishlists and "to try" lists can be shared with friends.

Connected to e-commerce

Our apps connect to the store's e-commerce platform, such as Shopify, for product data, customer accounts, order history and checkout.

Privacy first

Data is stored in the EU, customers can delete their account directly in the app, and we only collect what is needed.

What we do

We develop and operate mobile apps that are published under the store's own name on the App Store and Google Play. We handle design, development, hosting, updates and support. The store keeps its brand and its customer relationships.
