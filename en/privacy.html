Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy describes how W.E.C Labs AB (reg. no. 559508-2347, "we") processes personal data on this website and in the apps we develop. The Swedish version is the original; in case of differences, it prevails.

1. This website

This website uses no cookies, no visitor analytics and no tracking tools. Our web host may temporarily keep technical logs (such as IP address and time) to protect the service. If you email us, we keep your message and email address for as long as needed to handle your request, at most 24 months.

2. Our apps

We develop and operate apps that are published under a store's name. The store is the data controller for customer data in its app. We act as a data processor and process the data only on the store's behalf, under a data processing agreement. Each app also links to the store's own privacy policy.

Data processed by the apps

  • Account: your customer ID and email address from the store's customer account, when you sign in.
  • Your lists: products you save (variant, date added and purchase date), your own notes and where the product came from (scan, search or online order).
  • Order history: your previous online orders at the store, so you can choose which products to add.
  • Camera: used to read barcodes. The image does not leave your phone.
  • Photo search: if you photograph a package, the image is sent encrypted to an AI service that identifies the product. We do not store the image, and it is not used to train AI models. We count the number of searches per day to prevent abuse.
  • Reminders: created locally on your phone and can be turned off at any time.

The apps do not process sensitive personal data (such as health data), show no advertising and do not sell any data.

Purpose and legal basis

The data is processed to provide the app features you have asked for, i.e. to perform the contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Limiting the number of photo searches is based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where data is stored and who helps us

  • Supabase: database and server functions, stored in the EU.
  • Shopify (or the store's e-commerce platform): customer account, products, order history and checkout.
  • Anthropic: the AI service for photo search (USA). The transfer is protected by the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Apple and Google: app distribution.

How long

Your lists and notes are kept for as long as you have an account in the app. When you delete your account, they are deleted immediately.

3. Your rights

You have the right to access the data we process about you, to have it corrected or erased, to object to the processing and to receive your data (data portability). You can delete your account directly in the app under Profile → Delete account. If your question concerns a store's app, you can contact the store or us and we will help. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (imy.se).
