Support

Need help with an app we have developed? Email kontakt@weclabs.se. We normally reply within two business days.

Please tell us which app it concerns, which phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what happened. Questions about orders, deliveries, returns and loyalty rewards are answered by the store the app belongs to. Its contact details are in the app under Profile.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

You sign in with your customer account at the store. Enter your email address and you will receive a one-time code. If you do not have an account, one is created automatically.

The app cannot find my product when I scan it

The app only shows products in the store's range. Try searching by product name or brand instead, or photograph the package if the app has photo search.

How do I turn off reminders?

Go to Profile in the app and turn off reminders, or turn off notifications for the app in your phone's settings.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account in the app. Your saved lists and notes are deleted immediately, and a request to erase your customer account is sent to the store. You can also email us if you no longer have access to the app.

If I change phones, will my products come with me?

Yes. When you are signed in, your lists are saved to your account. Sign in to the app on your new phone.
