Support

Need help with an app we have developed? Email kontakt@weclabs.se. We normally reply within two business days.

Please tell us which app it concerns, which phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what happened. Questions about orders, deliveries and returns are answered by the store the app belongs to.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

You sign in with your customer account at the store the app belongs to.

How do I delete my account and my data?

You can delete your account directly in the app under Profile. You can also email us and we will help.

How do I turn off notifications?

Turn off notifications for the app in your phone's settings or in the app.
